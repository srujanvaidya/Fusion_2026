Guardian Platform Features & Capabilities Summary
==============================================

The Guardian platform is designed to revolutionize agriculture through space-grade intelligence and precision technology. We are building this platform to replace outdated manual surveys with real-time, automated data, providing government planners, insurance companies, and authorities with actionable insights for better resource allocation, disaster relief, and financial transparency.

Below is a detailed list of the core features and capabilities we have built:

1. Satellite Imagery & Crop Mapping
-----------------------------------
Harnesses multi-spectral and SAR satellite data to generate accurate crop maps across Maharashtra. The system processes optical imagery from Sentinel-2 and Landsat to compute vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, SAVI).
* Key Capabilities:
  - NDVI / EVI Analysis
  - Crop Classification
  - Area Estimation
  - Multi-temporal Tracking
  - SAR Data Fusion
  - Historical Comparison
* Real-Time Crop Mapping: Automatically identifies crop types (wheat, rice, sugarcane, etc.) and generates GIS-based crop distribution maps using Sentinel-2 data.

2. Drone Surveys & Aerial Analytics
-----------------------------------
Deploys advanced UAV technology for high-resolution, micro-level crop monitoring and precision agriculture. Provides capabilities that satellites cannot reach.
* Key Capabilities:
  - Precision Spraying
  - Damage Assessment (rapid assessment after natural disasters)
  - High-res Mapping
  - Thermal Imaging (for irrigation management)
  - Pest Detection (via multi-spectral imaging for plant health diagnosis)

3. GIS Mapping & Spatial Intelligence
-------------------------------------
Integrates disparate data sources into a unified Geographic Information System (GIS), serving as a robust spatial decision-support system for authorities.
* Key Capabilities:
  - Spatial Modeling
  - Soil Health Mapping
  - Network Analysis
  - Land Use Mapping
  - 3D Visualization
* GIS Dashboard for Authorities: Interactive map displaying village-level crop distribution, health status, groundwater availability, and disaster zones.

4. Mobile Geo-Tagged Data Collection
------------------------------------
Empowers field officers with a custom mobile application for real-time, ground-truthing to validate satellite imagery.
* Key Capabilities:
  - Offline Collection
  - GPS Tagging (captures time-stamped, GPS-verified photos)
  - Real-time Sync
  - Audit Trails (ensuring field-level transparency)
  - Custom Forms

5. Insurance Verification & Subsidy Distribution
------------------------------------------------
Revolutionizes agricultural financing by integrating satellite-based loss verification to ensure subsidies reach the right farmers.
* Key Capabilities:
  - Claims Processing
  - Fraud Detection
  - Direct Subsidies
  - Automated Verification
  - Payout Transparency
* Automated Insurance Verification: Compares before-and-after satellite data to detect crop damage and flood/drought impact. Outputs claim status, affected area, and confidence scores.

6. Yield Forecasting & Disaster Assessment
------------------------------------------
Utilizes machine learning models to forecast crop yields with high accuracy before harvest and assess damage post-disaster.
* Key Capabilities:
  - Yield Prediction
  - Disaster Mapping
  - Risk Assessment
  - Relief Optimization
  - Early Warning
* Rapid Impact Assessment: Enables government agencies to deploy relief resources effectively and minimize economic loss in the wake of natural disasters like droughts or floods.
