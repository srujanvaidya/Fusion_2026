About Aegis360. Founded by a team of ex-regulators, data scientists, and cybersecurity veterans, Aegis360 was built to solve the most complex fraud challenges in modern banking. We leverage behavioral AI and high-dimensional graph analytics to provide real-time, proactive intelligence against internal fraud, vendor risks, and sophisticated money laundering schemes.

Mission

Aegis360 exists to bridge the gap between regulatory compliance and cutting-edge AI, empowering financial institutions to detect, investigate, and prevent fraud before capital loss occurs.

Vision

To establish a unified, impenetrable layer of financial integrity across the global banking ecosystem.

 

We take pride in building dynamic, self-learning AI models that adapt to the ever-evolving tactics of institutional fraud.

The Minds Behind the Engine. The Aegis360 leadership team is comprised of seasoned financial intelligence officers, machine learning researchers, and enterprise architecture leaders. We understand the precise difficulties that banks face balancing friction-free customer experiences with stringent regulatory mandates and evolving insider threats.
